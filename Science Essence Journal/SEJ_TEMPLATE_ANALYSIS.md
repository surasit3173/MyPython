# Science Essence Journal (SEJ) Template Analysis & Manuscript Specification

## 1. Executive Summary
This document provides a detailed structural, typographic, and formatting analysis of the Science Essence Journal (SEJ) template (`template-SEJ-2023.docx` and published sample `1_16320-Article+Text_p1-11_f_2.pdf`) in comparison with the manuscript source (`Rainfall Occurrence–Intensity Heterogeneity and Its Implications for Provincial Extreme Rainfall Design.docx`).

The objective is to establish exact formatting rules for creating `Science Essence Journal/Rainfall_Occurrence_Intensity_Heterogeneity_SEJ_READY.docx` while preserving 100% of the original scientific content, mathematical formulas, dataset numbers, citations, figures, and tables.

## 2. Journal Template Formatting Specifications

### 2.1 General Document Properties
* **Paper Size**: A4 (21.0 cm x 29.7 cm / 8.27 in x 11.69 in)
* **Margins**: 1.0 inch (2.54 cm) on all sides (Top, Bottom, Left, Right)
* **Page Layout**: Single column throughout
* **Default Font**: Times New Roman

### 2.2 Title & Author Metadata Block
1. **Article Type**: `Research Article` — Times New Roman, 12 pt, Bold, Align Left / Justified.
2. **Title**: Times New Roman, 14 pt, Bold, Align Left. Capitalization as in original manuscript.
3. **Authors**: Times New Roman, 12 pt, Bold. E.g., `Surasit Punyawansiri*`
   - Asterisk (`*`) designates the Corresponding Author.
   - Subscript/superscript numbers indicate affiliations if multiple exist.
4. **Corresponding Author Indicator**: `(* for Corresponding author)` or footnote descriptor.
5. **Horizontal Divider Line 1**: `____________________________________________`
6. **Affiliations**: Times New Roman, 10 pt, Normal.
   - Format: `1 Office of Water Management and Hydrology, Royal Irrigation Department, Dusit, Bangkok, Thailand`
7. **Corresponding Author Email**: Times New Roman, 10 pt, Normal.
   - Format: `* Corresponding author, email: Surasit.pu@ku.th`
8. **Horizontal Divider Line 2**: `________________________________`

### 2.3 Abstract & Keywords Block
* **ABSTRACT Heading**: `ABSTRACT` — Times New Roman, 12 pt (or 14 pt), Bold.
* **Abstract Text**: Times New Roman, 12 pt, Normal, Single line spacing.
  - Must not exceed 250 words (Source manuscript abstract is 218 words -> **COMPLIANT**).
* **Keywords**: Times New Roman, 12 pt.
  - Heading: `Keywords (Times New Roman 12, pts Bold):` -> `Keywords:` in **Bold**.
  - Text: 3–5 keywords separated by comma `,`. Uppercase at the beginning of each keyword.
  - Source keywords: `Design rainfall, Frequency analysis, Rainfall intensity, Return period, Uttaradit` (5 keywords -> **COMPLIANT**).

### 2.4 Main Text Structure & Typography
* **Major Section Headings**: Times New Roman, 14 pt, Bold.
  - Required SEJ Sections:
    1. **Introduction**
    2. **Materials and Methods**
    3. **Results and Discussion**
    4. **Conclusions**
    5. **Acknowledgements**
    6. **References**
  - Additional Standard Sections present in manuscript:
    - **Author Contributions**
    - **Conflicts of Interest**
    - **Declaration of Generative AI**
* **Subsection Headings**: Times New Roman, 12 pt, Italic.
  - Format: `2.1 Study area and data`, `3.1 Occurrence–intensity structure of the network`, etc.
* **Body Paragraphs**:
  - Font: Times New Roman, 12 pt, Normal.
  - Line Spacing: Single (1.0).
  - Paragraph Indent: First line indent 0.5 inches (1.27 cm).
  - Alignment: Justified (Thai Justify / Justified).

### 2.5 Mathematical Equations
* Center-aligned or tab-aligned equation text.
* Right-aligned Arabic numerals in parentheses e.g. `(1)`, `(2)`, ..., `(15)`.
* Variable formatting: Italics for variables, plain text for functions/subscripts where appropriate.

### 2.6 Tables & Figures
* **Placement**: Placed directly in the text following their first mention.
* **Table Formatting**:
  - Caption above table: **Table 1** Table title in Times New Roman 12 pt, with **Table X** in **Bold**.
  - Table style: Clean open table style (horizontal borders at top, bottom, and below column headers; no vertical interior gridlines).
  - Header row: Times New Roman, 10–11 pt, Bold, centered/left-aligned.
  - Data rows: Times New Roman, 10–11 pt, Normal, numbers aligned right/center.
* **Figure Formatting**:
  - Center-aligned image insertion.
  - Caption below figure: **Figure 1** Legend text in Times New Roman 12 pt, with **Figure X** in **Bold**.

### 2.7 References Format
* **Style**: Vancouver Style, numbered sequentially using Arabic numerals in square brackets `[1]`, `[2]`, etc.
* **Citation in text**: Square brackets `[1]`, `[2]–[4]`, etc.
* **Reference List Format**: Times New Roman, 12 pt, Normal.
  - Authors (up to 6 authors listed before `et al.`).
  - Article title.
  - Journal Name (abbreviated according to standard ISO abbreviations).
  - Year; Volume(Issue): Pages.
  - DOI included where available.
