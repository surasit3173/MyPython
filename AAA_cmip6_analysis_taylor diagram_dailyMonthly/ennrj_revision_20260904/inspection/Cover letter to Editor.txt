[1] Subject: Submission of Manuscript entitled "Contrasting Seasonal Rainfall Trends under Predominantly Stable Annual Conditions: Evidence from Trend-Free Pre-Whitening Analysis in Prachuap Khiri Khan Province, Thailand"
[2] Dear Editor,
[3] I am pleased to submit my manuscript entitled "Contrasting Seasonal Rainfall Trends under Predominantly Stable Annual Conditions: Evidence from Trend-Free Pre-Whitening Analysis in Prachuap Khiri Khan Province, Thailand" for consideration as an Original Research Article in the Environment and Natural Resources Journal (EnNRJ).
[4] This study examined annual, wet-season, and dry-season rainfall trends at 12 rain-gauge stations in Prachuap Khiri Khan Province, coastal western Thailand, using daily rainfall records from 1981–2014 and a conditional Trend-Free Pre-Whitening Mann–Kendall (TFPW-MK) framework. The results show that while annual rainfall remained largely stable across the province, significant and opposing wet-season and dry-season trends emerged at several stations — most notably at station 500006, where significant wet-season drying coexisted with significant dry-season wetting despite no detectable annual trend. These findings are significant because they demonstrate that annual rainfall totals alone can conceal hydroclimatically important seasonal redistribution, a finding directly relevant to water-resource management, irrigation planning, and climate adaptation in monsoon-influenced coastal regions of Southeast Asia — a core concern of EnNRJ's scope. We believe this work will be of strong interest to the journal's readers.
[5] The manuscript has not been previously published and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. As the sole author, I have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to EnNRJ. I declare no competing interests.
