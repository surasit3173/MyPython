# APST manuscript template distillation

Source retained as read-only reference: `APST_format_reference.docx` (SHA-256 `587388a7c2b6b35978d14caebdddcdb11f6ceb3ffdd56f17c8e90e4a596ff1bc`).

## Page system

- A4 portrait: 8.2688 × 11.6944 inches.
- One continuous section, single column.
- Margins: 1.0 inch on all sides; header distance 0.4924 inch; footer distance 1.0 inch.
- Consecutive page number field at the top-right. The reference package contains PAGE fields in three header variants; preserve the field mechanism in the authored copy.
- Maximum length stated by the template: 15 pages.

## Typography and spacing

- Typeface: Times New Roman throughout.
- Article title: 12 pt, bold.
- Authors, affiliations, abstract, keywords, headings, body, tables, captions and references: 10 pt.
- Body paragraphs: justified, double-spaced. No paragraph spacing before/after unless required to keep a heading or caption with the following/preceding object.
- First-level section headings: numbered, 10 pt, bold (reference uses Heading 4 as the visual carrier).
- Second-level subsection headings: numbered, 10 pt, italic.
- Figure and table labels use a bold number after the word `Figure` or `Table`.

## Required content slots

1. Title.
2. Author line, affiliation(s), and corresponding-author email.
3. Abstract of no more than 250 words.
4. Four to eleven alphabetized keywords separated by commas.
5. Main body: Introduction; Materials and methods; Results; Discussion; Conclusion.
6. Ethical approval statement where applicable.
7. Generative-AI declaration when applicable.
8. Acknowledgements/funding.
9. Author contributions using CRediT roles.
10. Conflicts of interest.
11. References in English, numbered by first citation and formatted in Vancouver style; research-article maximum stated as 35 references.

## Tables, figures and references

- Tables and figures should sit near the first substantive callout, preferably at page top or bottom.
- Use native Word tables, no vertical rules, and only necessary horizontal rules.
- Figure captions appear below figures; table titles appear above tables.
- Composite panels use `(A)`, `(B)`, `(C)`, etc. at the upper-left of each panel.
- Figure legends should stay below 200 words. Visuals must remain legible in grayscale even when color is used online.
- In-text citations use Arabic numerals in square brackets, e.g. `[1]`, `[2,3]`, `[4-6]`.
